Our automation tool

What the YouTube integration does

WonderTales AI Automation is a private, owner-operated publishing tool. It uses YouTube API Services to verify the authorized channel, inspect its own upload records, and upload or schedule approved videos. It does not offer public Google sign-in, and viewers never need to connect their accounts.

Consent and control: Any YouTube authorization is requested from the channel owner through Google's permission screen. The owner can revoke access through Google Account third-party connections. Review our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service before authorizing the private tool.
