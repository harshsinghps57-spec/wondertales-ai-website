1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes how WonderTales AI Automation, an independent owner-operated storytelling and YouTube publishing tool, handles information when using YouTube API Services. It also covers this public informational website. Last updated: 15 November 2026.

This website is informational only. It has no sign-in form, account registration, analytics script, tracking pixels, or advertising cookies configured by us.

2. Information we access

With the channel owner's explicit Google OAuth consent, the private tool requests the youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes. It may access:

  • The authorized YouTube channel identifier, to ensure uploads go to the intended channel.
  • Information about the channel’s existing uploads, including video identifiers, tags, upload metadata, and visibility or scheduling status, to prevent duplicates and confirm scheduling.
  • Authorization tokens supplied by Google, to perform approved upload actions.

The tool generates and submits our own video files, captions, titles, descriptions and scheduling instructions. It is not intended to access viewers' Google accounts, private messages, or personal information about children.

3. How information is used

We use authorized YouTube information solely to authenticate the channel owner, confirm the channel identity, avoid duplicate uploads, upload approved stories, verify publishing status, and troubleshoot authorized publishing activity. We do not sell Google user data, use it for targeted advertising, or use it for unrelated profiling.

4. Storage, sharing and retention

Google OAuth credentials are intended to be stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets rather than in public source code. Operational upload identifiers and scheduling records may be stored in the private production repository. Diagnostic artifacts may be retained by the production workflow for up to 14 days; video metadata and upload records may persist until removed by the operator. Published videos are stored on YouTube under Google's services and policies.

Authorized information is processed by Google/YouTube and by service providers used to operate the owner's workflow, including GitHub for automation. The public site is hosted by the chosen hosting provider, which may handle routine technical logs such as IP addresses. We do not intentionally disclose authorized YouTube data to unaffiliated third parties.

5. Revoke access and request deletion

The authorized channel owner can revoke the app's access in Google Account third-party connections. The owner can also stop the automation and remove associated secrets in the private GitHub repository. To request removal of authorized information held by the operator, use the support contact below. We will remove associated Authorized Data as required by the YouTube API Services policies, including within seven calendar days after confirmed revocation where applicable.

Operator action required: before enabling production access, implement a process for detecting revocation, disabling tokens, and deleting associated authorized data. A Google Account revocation alone does not automatically erase copies stored elsewhere.

6. Children and family audiences

WonderTales AI creates content intended for children, but the website and publishing tool are not designed to collect personal information directly from children. The public website does not provide child profiles or interactive submissions. Video viewing and platform-level data handling are governed by YouTube and Google, not this website.

7. Security and your choices

We use least-privilege access where practical, do not ask for Google passwords, and intend to store access credentials privately. No online service can guarantee perfect security. You can review the permissions granted to the app, revoke them, or request deletion of authorized data from the operator.

See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles information, and the YouTube Terms of Service.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the website, production tools, legal obligations or authorized Google API use. Any material changes to how authorized data is used should be communicated and, where required, consent collected again.
